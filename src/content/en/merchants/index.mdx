## What every merchant integration does

- **Publishes an agent-readable product feed** on xpay's CDN — AI shoppers read it directly.
- **Adds Schema.org JSON-LD** (Product, Offer, BuyAction, ItemList) on PDPs and listings — so AI shoppers see your live prices and stock.
- **Serves `/llms.txt`** ([llmstxt.org](https://llmstxt.org)) on your domain — the Markdown discovery file AI agents and LLMs look for.
- **Allows the right bots** in `robots.txt` — `GPTBot`, `ChatGPT-User`, `ClaudeBot`, `Google-Extended`, `PerplexityBot`, `CCBot`, `OAI-SearchBot`, `Amazonbot`.
- **Implements the agentic commerce protocols you opt into** — ACP per-session checkout endpoints, UCP OAuth Identity Linking with RFC 9421 signed requests, AP2 mandate acceptance. Pick one or all; they compose.
- **Cart deeplink** — agents mint a one-click "Buy" link that lands the buyer on your existing checkout, pre-filled.
- **Non-custodial** — payment runs through your existing gateway (Stripe / WooPayments / PayPal / Square / etc.). xpay never touches checkout or sees buyer PII.

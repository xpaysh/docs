# Verification

Get a verified badge on your provider profile to build trust with users.

## Verification Badge

Verified providers get a checkmark badge displayed on:
- Your provider page at [xpay.tools](https://xpay.tools)
- Tool listings in search results
- The `xpay_discover` meta-tool results

## Requirements

To get verified, providers must:

1. **Active server** — Your MCP server must be responsive and serving valid tool results
2. **Consistent uptime** — Demonstrated reliability over a minimum period
3. **Accurate descriptions** — Tool names, descriptions, and pricing match actual behavior
4. **Valid identity** — Website or GitHub profile linked to your account

## How to Apply

1. Go to [xpay.tools](https://xpay.tools) → Account → Creator Settings
2. Click **Apply for Verification**
3. Provide your website URL, GitHub profile, or other identity proof
4. The xpay team reviews and responds within a few business days

## Benefits

- Increased visibility in search results
- Higher trust from users
- Eligible for featured placement in collections
